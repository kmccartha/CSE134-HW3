Study smarter around your shifts

Break a project into steps

Large assignments feel smaller when each step fits in one block.

  1. Read the spec twice and list every requirement.
  2. Sketch the design or the page structure.
  3. Build the smallest working version.
  4. Test it, then fix one problem at a time.
  5. Re-read the spec and submit early.
Estimate each step in hours, then add 25% for surprises.

Exam prep

  • Start one week out with spaced practice, a little each day.
  • Work old problems without notes first.
  • Explain tricky topics aloud in your own words.
  • Schedule exams around shifts before the week begins.

Focus sessions

Pick one task, silence notifications and work for 25 minutes, then rest for 5. Add these blocks to your weekly plan.
